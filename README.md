## ✨ Overview & Core Architecture

Clackey delivers the tactile rhythm of mechanical typewriters without compromising input velocity or device security:

- **Acoustic Sound Engine:** Low-latency `SoundPool` playback with round-robin strike variations, cushioned spacebar damping, and authentic mechanical carriage return bells.
- **Mechanical Plate Layout:** Distinctive circular typewriter keycaps paired with CNC plate styling, dual-channel backlight halos, and adaptive landscape geometry.
- **Strict On-Device Privacy:** All word suggestions, personal user dictionaries, and typing histories remain strictly on your physical device. Zero background analytics, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry.
- **Smart Clipboard:** Quick clipboard suggestions with auto-clear privacy timers (30 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes) to protect sensitive pins and addresses.
- **Customization & Themes:** Curated light, blush, chrome, and deep mocha color palettes with high-contrast accessibility.
