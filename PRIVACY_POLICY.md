# Privacy Policy for Clackey – Typewriter Keyboard

**Effective Date:** September 23, 2026  
**Application:** Clackey – Typewriter Keyboard (`com.clackey.keyboard`)  
**Developer:** Clackey Team  
**Contact:** fari.dev.contact@gmail.com

## 1. Our Privacy Promise to You

At Clackey, we believe that typing on your phone is deeply personal. Your thoughts, private messages, passwords, and creative notes belong solely to you.

We built Clackey with a fundamental commitment: **complete on-device privacy**. Clackey does not collect, record, track, or share your keystrokes, personal messages, passwords, or personal data. 

---

## 2. What We Never Collect or Store

We maintain a strict zero-collection standard for your typing activity:

- **No Keystroke Recording:** We never record, log, or store the letters, words, or numbers you type. When you press a key, the letter is passed directly to the app you are currently using and is never saved by Clackey.
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers. You do not need to create an account or sign in to use Clackey.
- **No Tracking or Analytics:** Clackey contains no background analytics tools, diagnostic beacons, or user tracking code. We do not monitor how fast you type, what words you write, or how often you use the keyboard.
- **No Advertising or Profiling:** We do not display ads, sell data, or build behavioral advertising profiles based on your activity.

---

## 3. How Your Data Stays on Your Device

All personalizations and temporary typing conveniences exist solely on your phone:

### A. Your Personal Dictionary
- Any custom words or shorthand expansions you add are saved locally on your phone.
- Your dictionary never leaves your device and is never synced to any remote servers. You can view, edit, or delete these words anytime in Clackey Settings.

### B. Clipboard History & Auto-Clear
- If you use Clackey's clipboard feature, recently copied text items and smart chips (like phone numbers or links) are stored strictly inside the app on your phone.
- **Automatic Privacy Clearing:** You can set copied items to automatically erase after 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 5 minutes so sensitive information (such as two-factor codes or addresses) isn't left sitting in clipboard history.
- You can clear your clipboard history at any time with a single tap.

### C. Passwords & Sensitive Fields
- When you type into password fields, PIN entries, or incognito browsing tabs, Clackey automatically suppresses word predictions and clipboard suggestions to keep your sensitive input protected.

---

## 4. Internet Access & Expressive GIF Search

Clackey is designed to work 100% offline. The only feature that connects to the internet is the optional GIF search powered by GIPHY:

- **Only When You Search:** An internet connection is used exclusively when you actively open the GIF panel and enter a search query.
- **No Background Typing Sent:** Only the specific word you type into the GIF search box is sent to find animations. Your normal typing, messages, clipboard history, and identity are never transmitted.
- Use of the GIF search feature is subject to [GIPHY's Privacy Policy](https://giphy.com/privacy).

---

## 5. Optional Community Feedback & Feature Requests

Clackey provides an optional in-app feedback and product roadmap portal (powered by [Fedo](https://getfedo.com)):

- **User-Initiated Only:** The feedback portal is only contacted when you deliberately open **Settings → Feedback & Community Roadmap** or submit a suggestion from **Help & Support**.
- **Data Submitted:** Only the text you explicitly provide (such as your feedback title, description, or optional contact info) and anonymous vote counts are transmitted to Fedo to display feature requests and bug reports.
- **Zero Keyboard Access:** The feedback portal is completely separate from keyboard typing. Your keystrokes, personal messages, and clipboard data are never accessible or shared.
- For more information on Fedo's privacy standards, visit [getfedo.com](https://getfedo.com).

---

## 6. Device Permissions Explained

Android requires keyboard apps to request certain permissions to function. Here is why Clackey requests them:

| Permission | What It Does for You | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **Input Method Service** | Allows Android to use Clackey as your active keyboard across all your apps. | Required for all keyboards |
| **Internet Access** | Used only to retrieve animated GIFs when you search for them in the GIF tab. | Optional (Typing works 100% offline) |
| **Photos & Media** | Only used if you turn on the optional feature to preview recently copied screenshots in your clipboard bar. Your photos never leave your device. | Optional (Can be turned off anytime) |

---

## 7. Your Rights & Data Control

You are in total control of your data at all times:

- **Delete Your Data:** You can delete your custom dictionary words or wipe your clipboard history directly from the Clackey Settings screen.
- **Full App Reset:** Clearing Clackey's storage in your Android System Settings permanently erases all local settings, words, and history.
- **No Cloud Account:** Because we do not store your data on external servers, there are no remote accounts or server profiles to delete.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Clackey does not collect personal information from any user, including children. The application is family-friendly and safe for typists of all ages.
